Architecture Note Why SerpApi Powers TenderRadar's Core Discovery

Indian public procurement (over ₹50 Lakh Crore annually) is fragmented across dozens of legacy government portals (CPPP, GeM, IREPS, State PWDs) that enforce session cookies (;jsessionid=...) and aggressive CAPTCHAs. Traditional scrapers break continuously. TenderRadar uses SerpApi's Google Search API with domestic India localization (gl=in, location=India) to simultaneously index notices across 8+ official domains without CAPTCHA locks, caching results via SQLite to protect monthly API quotas.
