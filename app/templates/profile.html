How the Bid Advisor Evaluates Tenders

  1. Capacity Screening: Matches bids against your turnover, contract value range, and available EMD budget.
  2. Technical Fit Analysis: Analyzes the scope of work against your core trade categories, domains, and certifications.
  3. Risk & Exemption Detection: Highlights tight deadlines (<7 days) and identifies MSME/Startup fee exemptions under Govt procurement rules.
  4. Instant Recalculation: Updating your profile immediately re-scores and re-ranks all active tenders.

Pro-Tip for Indian MSMEs

Under the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order 2012, registered MSMEs are exempt from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) and tender document fees on Central Government portals (GeM & CPPP).
