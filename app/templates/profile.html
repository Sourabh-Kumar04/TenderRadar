⚡ How Bid Advisor Works

  1. Deterministic Pre-Filter: Tenders are filtered by domain keywords, contract value bracket, and EMD capacity.
  2. DeepSeek AI Reasoning: Evaluates tender RFP nuances, technical requirements, and contractor fit.
  3. Risk & Opportunity Flags: Flags tight deadlines (<7 days), liquidity risks, and MSME fee waivers.
  4. Decision Caching: Results are cached by profile hash. When you edit your profile here, verdicts recalculate instantly!

💡 Pro-Tip for Indian MSMEs

Under the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order 2012, registered MSMEs are exempt from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) and tender document fees on Central Government portals (GeM & CPPP).
